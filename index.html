ABOUT THIS WORLD

A place to explore.

A contemporary reconstruction of Clawdd Glas and Howey village up to the Village Hall, near Llandrindod Wells, Powys — real SRTM topography (fitted to surveyed elevation) draped with satellite imagery.

Building positions use OpenStreetMap data retrieved September 2026. Houses carry rendered and brick facades with slate gable roofs; heights and window patterns are estimated, with unseen sides inferred. Trees, lanes and the valley floor are reconstructed planting and surfacing, not a survey of any individual property.

Orbit freely, drop to the lanes at eye level, or take the flying tour around the valley.
